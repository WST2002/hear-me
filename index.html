Palak a blink becomes a voice

Palak lets someone who cannot speak or move choose a phrase with a long blink, and reads it aloud.

  • The camera is used only while a session is running. You can stop it at any time.
  • Video is processed on this computer. Palak records nothing and saves nothing.
  • SDK telemetry is turned off. The Presage SDK checks the API key online.
  • Palak is a communication aid, not a medical device.
